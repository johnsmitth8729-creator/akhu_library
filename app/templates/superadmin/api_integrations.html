API Texnik Hujjatlari & Endpointlar
GET /api/rankings/students

Kitobxon talabalarning umumiy reytingi (Top Readers & Borrowers birlashtirilgan). Parametrlar: limit (masalan, ?limit=50).

curl -H "X-API-Key: KALIT" "{{ base_api_url }}/api/rankings/students?limit=50"
GET /api/rankings/student/{passport}

Bitta talabaning kitobxonlik ko'rsatkichlari, mutolaa sahifalari, kitoblar tarixi va uning umumiy reytingdagi o'rni.

curl -H "X-API-Key: KALIT" "{{ base_api_url }}/api/rankings/student/AD9456428"
GET /api/competitions/rankings

Tanlovlar ro'yxati, ishtirokchilar va urinishlar soni, va har bir tanlovning TOP 3 g'oliblari (pasport raqami bilan).

curl -H "X-API-Key: KALIT" "{{ base_api_url }}/api/competitions/rankings?status=active"
GET /api/competitions/{id}/ranking

Aynan bitta tanlovning to'liq ishtirokchilar reytingi, o'rtacha ball, o'tish foizi va sertifikat verifikatsiya kodlari.

curl -H "X-API-Key: KALIT" "{{ base_api_url }}/api/competitions/1/ranking"
GET /api/competitions/student/{passport}

Talabaning tanlovlardagi yutuqlari, medallar (🥇🥈🥉), nishonlar, sertifikatlar va barcha tanlov urinishlari tarixi.

curl -H "X-API-Key: KALIT" "{{ base_api_url }}/api/competitions/student/AD9456428"
GET /api/competitions/leaderboard/overall

Universitet bo'yicha barcha tanlovlarning umumiy chempionlar reytingi (medallar va tanlov ballari vazni asosida).

curl -H "X-API-Key: KALIT" "{{ base_api_url }}/api/competitions/leaderboard/overall?limit=50"
